Ontario PHU historical incidence: identification audit

Research repository: source code and reproducible workflow

No verified causal policy effect is established. The checked-in CSV has not been independently reconciled with original Government of Ontario published records. The rationale for classifying five public health units as treated and 29 as comparison is not documented against a verified, differential intervention. The meaning and units of the incidence field have not been independently verified.

What has actually been checked

The checked-in panel comprises 4,760 PHU-week records, 34 coded PHUs and 140 consecutive weekly observation dates from March 30, 2020 through November 28, 2022. These are observations from the repository file, not a claim of successful government source reconciliation.
